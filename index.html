How to use this app

🎴
Flip Cards & Views Click anywhere on a card to flip it over. Use the view toggle to switch between 3D Flashcards and a Compact List.

⭐
Starred Words & Practice Star challenging words to create a custom study set. Filter by Starred to focus only on those terms in both the list and the Listening Game.
🔴
Self-Assessment Ratings Rate card difficulty with 🟢 (Easy), 🟡 (Medium), or 🔴 (Hard) to track your progress and sort cards by difficulty.
